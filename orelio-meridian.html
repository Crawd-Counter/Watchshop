Calibre O‑12

A movement we build, not buy.

Most watches at this price carry an outsourced movement finished to look proprietary. Ours isn't. Calibre O‑12 is designed, machined, and regulated in our own workshop — 187 components, hand-bevelled bridges, and a free-sprung balance that holds ‑2/+4 seconds a day, tested over fifteen positions before it ever reaches a case.

You can watch it work: the case back is open, and the rotor is engraved with the coordinates of our workshop.

Materials

What it's made from, and why.

Case — 316L steel

Machined from a single billet, then hand-polished between brushed lugs so the case catches light only where it should.

Strap — chestnut leather

Vegetable-tanned in a family tannery outside Florence, then hand-stitched with waxed saddle thread that darkens with wear.

Crystal — sapphire

Domed and anti-reflective on both faces, hard enough to resist scratching from anything short of another sapphire.
